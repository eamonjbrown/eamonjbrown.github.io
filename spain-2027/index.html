    Logistics

    • Flights. Boston to Barcelona is nonstop on Level, Iberia and Delta. Barcelona to Asturias has about 5 nonstops a day on Vueling and Volotea. Bilbao to Edinburgh is a Vueling nonstop on some days only, so check the 2027 schedule. United's Bilbao to Newark nonstop is scheduled for 21 May to 22 Sep 2027.
    • Driving. Spain asks US drivers for an International Driving Permit (from AAA). Both car hires are one-way, so expect a drop fee. In the UK you drive on the left, and automatics need booking early.
    • Rentals. In Barcelona and San Sebastián, book only listings that show a tourist licence number. Both cities are cancelling unlicensed rentals.
    • Entry. The UK needs an ETA for each traveller. The EU's ETIAS is due around late 2026, so check it before June.
